# 03 — Solusi Existing dan Kekurangannya

Hasil search Firecrawl 23 Sep 2026. Hanya yang ada source; di luar ini = belum terverifikasi.

## Klinik / Dental

### Weave
Solusi: VoIP + reminder terjadwal + review + payments, all-in-one single-location.
Kekurangan:
- Single-step reminder, tidak conditional. Tidak eskalasi channel/pesan jika pasien tidak confirm.
- Scheduling terbatas ke reminder/confirm; tidak support direct patient-initiated scheduling di kalender PMS.
- Gap: conditional multi-step flow, multi-location management, sentiment routing review.
Source:
- https://ustechautomations.com/resources/blog/automate-weave-alternatives-for-dental-practices-2026
- https://practicesignal.com/dental/compare/weave-vs-nexhealth-vs-solutionreach

### NexHealth
Solusi: scheduling-first, real-time availability, forms masuk chart, reminder akurat walau jadwal last-minute berubah. Month-to-month fleksibel.
Kekurangan:
- Keluhan lag/sync failures; G2 headline tinggi tapi support sub-score perlu dicek.
- Overpriced untuk kebutuhan reminder-only; per-location Mitgliedschaft memberatkan multi-site.
- Laporan user: tidak sebagus demo, sebagian switch back ke Weave setelah 6 bulan (kasus Denticon).
Source:
- https://practicesignal.com/dental/compare/weave-vs-nexhealth-vs-solutionreach
- https://www.facebook.com/groups/1594069521337374/posts/1695586657852326/

### Solutionreach
Solusi: recall automation (klaim 18-24% lebih banyak hygiene appt/bulan vs manual), scheduling, intake, surveys, multi-unit.
Kekurangan:
- Kontrak tahunan auto-renew, cancel window 15 hari; keluhan aggressive billing / triple price.
- 500+ integrasi tapi depth read/write variatif — wajib verifikasi per PMS.
- BBB tidak akreditasi, 14 complaints unanswered (per PracticeSignal 2026).
- Single-step review request, no sentiment routing (vs Birdeye).
Source:
- https://ustechautomations.com/resources/blog/automate-weave-alternatives-for-dental-practices-2026
- https://practicesignal.com/dental/compare/weave-vs-nexhealth-vs-solutionreach
- https://emitrr.com/blog/solutionreach-vs-weave/

## Salon / Spa

### Fresha
Solusi: booking layer gratis untuk salon.
Kekurangan: no AI receptionist (tidak jawab telpon/SMS/WhatsApp saat stylist mid-service); komisi 20% new-client marketplace + 2.19%+$0.20; CRM tipis (no color formula/allergy notes).
Source: https://bookingpro.ai/blog/best-salon-software-2026/

### Mindbody
Solusi: enterprise spa/wellness groups di kontrak legacy.
Kekurangan: enterprise pricing + kontrak; voice AI dangkal koneksinya ke scheduling/POS; migrasi keluar painful (export client data/notes/transaksi tidak friendly).
Source: https://bookingpro.ai/blog/best-salon-software-2026/

### Mangomint
Solusi: UI modern + support positif, automations (reminder, rebooking prompt, win-back).
Kekurangan: no native AI agent voice/SMS/WhatsApp inbound; US-only; learning curve + isu billing/customization menurut review.
Source:
- https://bookingpro.ai/blog/best-salon-software-2026/
- https://www.portraitcare.com/post/mangomint-vs-fresha

### Boulevard
Solusi: high-end appointment businesses, client management kuat.
Kekurangan: AI narrower dari marketing (automation flows, bukan end-to-end voice/SMS/WhatsApp); pricing tidak publish + minimum seat; mobile app reported buggy (iOS/Android rating rendah).
Source:
- https://bookingpro.ai/blog/best-salon-software-2026/
- https://www.mangomint.com/go/switch-from-boulevard/

## HVAC / Field Service

### Jobber
Solusi: drag-and-drop calendar 1-15 techs, rekomendasi untuk yang outgrow HCP tapi belum siap ServiceTitan. Trial 14 hari.
Kekurangan: reporting terlalu basic; kustomisasi form/workflow terbatas; fitur esensial dikunci tier atas; kurang untuk dispatch kompleks.
Source:
- https://contractorplus.app/blog/housecall-pro-vs-jobber-vs-servicetitan
- https://fieldservicesoftware.io/comparisons/housecall-pro-vs-jobber-vs-servicetitan/

### Housecall Pro
Solusi: fastest deploy, entry cost rendah, scheduling simple untuk fast entry + quick reschedule, online booking 24/7.
Kekurangan: reporting weakest of three; thin untuk structured sales process; not customizable (review user); limit terasa di 10-15 techs (dispatch, sales tracking, multi-location reporting).
Source:
- https://fieldservicesoftware.io/comparisons/housecall-pro-vs-jobber-vs-servicetitan/
- https://www.fieldpulse.com/resources/blog/servicetitan-vs-housecall-pro

### ServiceTitan
Solusi: automation + scale, advanced scheduling multi-view + route optimization, robust untuk revenue >$2-3M.
Kekurangan: learning curve steep; mobile comprehensive tapi butuh training; small shop bayar 4-5x Jobber tapi pakai ~30%; poor fit <5 techs; onboarding rep overworked (review Capterra); keluhan cancel nightmare + pricebook barebones.
Source:
- https://contractorplus.app/blog/housecall-pro-vs-jobber-vs-servicetitan
- https://fieldservicesoftware.io/comparisons/housecall-pro-vs-jobber-vs-servicetitan/

## Pola kekurangan lintas solusi

1. Reminder single-step tanpa conditional escalation.
2. AI sempit (automation flow) bukan agen inbound end-to-end.
3. Integrasi banyak tapi dangkal; write-back per sistem tidak dijamin.
4. Harga opaque + kontrak kaku + migrasi keluar sulit.
5. Reporting lemah di tier SMB; enterprise overkill untuk tim kecil.

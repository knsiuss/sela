# 01 — Executive Summary

## 1.1 Opportunity Overview

Source: https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/appointment-scheduling-software-market-108614

[Fakta] No-show rata-rata ~23% dari 105 studi (Dantas et al. 2018). Baseline 10 klinik VA 18.8% selama 12 tahun (Kheirkhah et al. 2016). Biaya ~$196/no-show (2008 USD) / rule-of-thumb $200/slot.
Source: https://www.etisia.com/no-show-statistics, https://clinekthealth.com/blog/average-no-show-rate-and-cost

[Interpretasi] Reminder 1-arah sudah komoditas. Gap yang tersisa: tangkap balasan teks bebas kapan pun, rebook ke slot live, refill slot kosong dari waitlist tanpa manusia. Bukti: praktik kirim 200 SMS/hari tetap terima 40+ inbound reschedule call besok paginya; no-show 5-30% persisten walau reminder jalan.
Source: https://www.bland.ai/blog/voice-ai-for-appointment-reminders-and-rescheduling

## 1.2 Key Findings

1. Lupa penyebab #1 (33-44% self-report), tapi penyebab sistemik: jadwal inflexible, no reminder, akses reschedule sulit.
2. 41% call masuk di luar 8AM-5PM; 23% inbound call miss rata-rata (solo 30%+); 85% tidak call back.
3. Cancel 5-30%; 81.3% dental provider sebut short-notice cancel barrier utama kapasitas. Waitlist otomatis klaim isi 70-90% cancel.
4. Harga incumbent SMB: $149-500/mo. Weave ~$249 + $750 setup; NexHealth ~$350/location; Solutionreach $199 list / $400-800 reported mid-market; salon $25-350; HVAC $49-349 (ServiceTitan custom premium).
5. SEA = WhatsApp-first; US klinik/HVAC = SMS + voice masih dominan.

## 1.3 Strategic Recommendation

Fokus 1 vertikal + 1 PMS/FSM dulu (contoh: fisio + Cliniko, dental + Open Dental, salon + Mangomint, HVAC + Jobber). MVP SMS/WhatsApp-first: reschedule + cancel + waitlist refill + after-hours. Voice setelah write-back stabil.

## 1.4 Investment Thesis (Why Now)

- AI conversational + calendar/PMS API matang; Meta WhatsApp Cloud API + MCP permudah setup.
- Incumbent mahal/kaku (kontrak tahunan, setup fee, pricing opaque) → ruang untuk produk accessible + outcome-based.
- Denda no-show ($25-75, dipakai 42% praktik) terbukti hanya symptom fix; pasar butuh friksi rendah.

## 1.5 Expected Outcomes (12-24 months)

[Asumsi — harus divalidasi pilot] Target per lokasi: reschedule completion tanpa manusia >60%, slot recovery <15 menit, recovered revenue terukur dalam 30 hari. Bukan janji; metrik pilot yang menentukan lanjut/tidak.
